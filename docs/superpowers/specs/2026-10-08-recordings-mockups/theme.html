How it works in both versions
  • Light / Dark / System switch in the top bar (shown top right). It follows your OS setting until you choose otherwise, and each browser remembers your choice.
  • Orange never carries text in light mode. On white it only reaches 3.2:1, which is fine for markers and outlines but too faint for words. Blue text measures 6.6:1 on white and 6.3:1 in dark mode, both comfortably above the 4.5:1 that normal text needs.
  • The app gets its own _brand.yml as the single source for these colours, and the build turns it into CSS variables for both modes. Before building that, I'll check brand.yml's current dark-mode support in its docs.
  • Fonts are self-hosted, as on your site, so the app never contacts Google Fonts. (This mockup does load them from Google Fonts, purely for the preview.)
