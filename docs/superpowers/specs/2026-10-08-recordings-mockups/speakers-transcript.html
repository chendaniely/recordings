    Design notes

    • Three chip styles (§12.6a): confirmed, by you or by Plaud, is solid; inferred is dashed in --muted-foreground with "· auto"; unnamed is plain muted text. The transcript shows no scores; they are only in the strip, on hover.
    • Chips sit in their own column on desktop, so the hover chip on a mid-run line appears without moving any text. The built app puts the name above the text; on a phone it stays there, on the first line of each run only, because phones have no hover.
    • Each line is an <li> holding two sibling buttons, one to seek and one for the chip. That is the fix §12.6a asks for, with no button inside a button. Only one line is in the Tab order (roving focus), and the arrow keys move between lines.
    • The picker lists "In this recording" first, numbered for 1–9. An unnamed label there says "same voice → Unknown 1", and picking it creates that placeholder (§7.6: recognise: false, people file first). Search puts existing matches before "Create …", the same fix as the tag picker (§12.2). It closes on choice.
    • How scope maps to the file (§7.6): "All N lines from this voice" writes labels. "Only 3:12" and "These 7 lines" write spans in media time. "Not Alex" writes not and brings up the next-best match as a Medium suggestion in the strip.
    • The range demo is real: at 7:54 Dan says "Sam, you had this working". The seven lines after it are under Speaker 2, Alex's voice. That is two people in one label, and spans are the fix.
    • Three kinds of feedback: the toast for your own change, which fades and has Undo; the "Voice matching updated 2 labels" banner on the open recording, which stays up (§12.6a); and "Found Alex in 23 older recordings", which turns up about 2 s after you confirm Alex and also stays up until dismissed.
    • Speaker bands: one lane per resolved speaker, in blues for named people and greys for unnamed ones. Orange is used only for the playhead and the line playing now (§12.6). Highlighting a label dims the other lanes.
    • On a phone, tapping a line opens a bottom sheet with "Play from", "Select lines…" and the same picker. "Select lines" turns taps into a multi-select, in place of Shift-click.
    • Not shown here: a single-speaker recording, which per §12.6a hides the strip and says one voice was found, and the "Plaud now says X" review item.

    Open questions for Dan

    • How should a Plaud-named label look? It is confirmed, but derived. §12.6a says confirmed chips are solid, yours or Plaud's, yet its verb is "Not Dan", not "Unassign". As drawn, the chip looks exactly like one you set, so you can't predict the verb until you open the picker. The source shows only in the strip ("Plaud") and in the picker's subtitle. The Show "· Plaud" mockup control tries a muted suffix like "· auto". Which do you want?
    • Medium matches. §7.6 sends Medium to Suggestions rather than applying it, while the strip shows "High or Medium". The mockup shows a Medium match only in the strip ("maybe Sam Lee", with Confirm and Reject), and the transcript still reads "Speaker 3". Is that right? Until auto_threshold is calibrated every match is Medium, so at first no "· auto" chip would appear at all.
    • "Not Alex" for one line or a range has no form in the file. not exists only per label, and a span needs a person. The mockup applies "Not Alex" to the whole voice, says so, and hides it for a range. Should there be a "not this person" span?
    • Sweep banner, or a pinned generation? §7.6 says an open recording pins its generation; §12.6a says a sweep never silently changes the open recording. The mockup applies the update and offers Undo, which is the wording in the task. The other reading holds the update until you click "Show update". And what does Undo record: a not, which is a decision, or just an index rollback?
    • "Unknown 1" is your decision, so its chip is solid, but solid reads as a real name. Does a placeholder need its own look, for example italics?
    • Picking an unnamed label for a named one, say "Speaker 5" on Alex's auto label: the mockup makes both "Unknown 1" and drops Alex. Would "Speaker 5 → Alex" be the better reading?
    • Keys 1–9 pick entries in "In this recording", which include unnamed labels as well as people. They work only while the search box is empty, so that digits can still be typed. The current speaker keeps its number but is disabled, unless it is an auto tag, where picking it confirms the tag.
    • Space plays and pauses when focus is on a line, so Enter is what seeks to a line. Is that split acceptable?
