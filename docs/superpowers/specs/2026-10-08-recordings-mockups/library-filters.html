Library: finding recordings

    Design notes

    25 made-up recordings: the four demo recordings plus weekly syncs, COURSE 101 lectures, office hours and conference calls from Aug 31 to Oct 8 2026. Every count is computed from that list, so the sidebar, chips, menus and empty-state numbers agree.

    Filtering (§12.1, §12.6a)

    • Sidebar items act as filters. Untagged adds an Untagged chip, and All recordings means Clear. Needs attention (failed or stale) adds a chip too. Review opens its own queue, which isn't in this mockup.
    • Click replaces, ⌘/Ctrl-click adds. Clicking a tag or a person filters by that one alone, and clicking it again removes it. ⌘/Ctrl-click (or ⌘/Ctrl+Enter from the keyboard) adds it to what's there. That's the Finder rule from §12.2. On a Mac, Ctrl-click is a right-click, so there it has to be ⌘.
    • How filters combine: AND across kinds (Untagged, tags, people, date, search). Two tags also combine with AND. People use all of by default or any of, switched inside the People chip: "Dan + Alex · all ▾". The menu shows the count for each.
    • Untagged and a user tag can't both be true, so choosing one drops the other. Note types still combine with Untagged.
    • The filter bar: every active filter is a chip, Untagged and search text included, and Clear sits at the end. Small "and"s between chips spell out the logic. They're hidden on a phone.
    • The date chip ("Oct 1–15") comes from the date button in the list header, which D also opens. Only presets are offered here, because the calendar has its own mockup.

    People

    • The People facet lists named people, most frequent first, with inferred matches split out: "Alex Kim 16 (6 unconfirmed)". It shows the top five, then a Show all link.
    • Where it sits: right under All, Untagged, Needs attention and Review, above the tag tree, in the sidebar and in the phone sheet alike. §12.1 lists it last, but there it falls below the fold at laptop heights, because the sidebar would need about 1000 px.
    • Inferred matches are counted by default. A person's count includes recordings where only voice matching found them, and a person filter includes those too (Dan, 2026-10-08). The facet keeps the split visible. The list header says how many results came only from voice and offers Confirmed only.
    • Unnamed placeholders ("Unknown 1", "Unknown 2") are left out of the facet and can't be filtered on. A muted "Unnamed (2) left out" line links to People.
    • Rows show who is in the recording: "Dan, Alex +2". Filtered people come first, in bold. Inferred names are muted and dashed, and unnamed voices are muted. The dashes are drawn in --muted-foreground, as §12.6a asks. The built .chip.auto still uses --border.
    • Untagged plus a person is allowed. It shows a triage line with Select all, after which T would open the tag picker (see tagging-v3.html).

    Search

    • Typing "ale" shows "Person: Alex Kim" in its own group, above the title and transcript hits. Choosing it turns it into a People chip and clears the text.
    • Why the person row stands apart: the transcript hits for "ale" include the spoken word "Alex", plus "scale" and "stale".
    • Enter with nothing highlighted keeps the text as a search chip. ↓ moves into the suggestions.

    Empty state and phone

    • The empty state reads "Nothing matches · Any of: 1 · All dates: 2 · Without work/conference: 1 · Without people: 1 · Clear". Each option loosens one filter and shows the count it would give. Options that would still give nothing are hidden.
    • On a phone, a bottom tab bar holds Library, Tags, People, Insights and More, with Add and Status under More. The sidebar becomes a Filters sheet, and its People section starts collapsed. In the sheet a tap toggles a filter, since there is no ⌘-click on a phone. Chips scroll sideways, and a row opens the pane over the list, with a Back button.

    Build notes

    • Rows here are valid HTML: each row is an <li> holding a single <button> for the title, so people and chips can become buttons later. The built RecordingList rows put <div>s inside a <button>, as TranscriptTab lines do. That is invalid HTML, and the real build fixes both (stage 3a, §12.6a).
    • Orange appears only on the active page and the stale dot (and the placeholder playhead). Filter chips and selection are blue.

    Open questions for Dan

    • Facet order: is People above Tags acceptable, or should it go back to the bottom with tag folders collapsed instead?
    • Sidebar counts are for the whole library, not narrowed by the other filters. Narrowed counts would preview what ⌘-click gives, but they shift as you click. Which do you want?
    • Picking a second tag: §12.1 says two tags combine with AND but not how you pick the second one. This mockup uses the people rule (⌘-click adds). Is that right?
    • Unnamed voices: since placeholders are left out of the facet, should "Has unnamed voices" be a filter of its own for triage?
    • Confirmed only: keep the switch, or drop it, given that inferred matches are included by default?
    • Tag suggestions: should search also suggest tags ("Tag: work/conference") alongside people?
