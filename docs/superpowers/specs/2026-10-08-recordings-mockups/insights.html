Design notes

  • Order follows §12.6b: four cards, the full-width heatmap, the punch card beside "Averages and busiest times", model usage beside trends, Speakers, then the footer.
  • Python computes, React draws. One compute() stands in for @reactive_output("insights"). Its inputs are the date control, the tag chips, the hours/count switch and the year, sent with useShinyInput. Hover, focus and the day details stay in the browser and never round-trip.
  • Recalculating: any input change keeps the old charts at half opacity under a thin "Recalculating in Python…" bar (useShinyOutputStatus), for about 400 ms here and 850 ms in the Pages demo, then swaps them. There is no skeleton and no layout jump. The footer timing is made up: 24–68 ms, or 160–420 ms with Pyodide.
  • The hours/count switch changes the heatmap, punch card, trends and "who you meet most", never the cards. Model usage stays words and notes, and talk time stays hours.
  • Heatmap: one blue scale at the brand hue, never orange, with fixed steps. Out-of-range days are dimmed and future days are blank. The week starts on Monday. The streaks are in the caption. Arrow keys move a week or a day, Enter opens the day, and each cell has an aria-label. "Open in Library →" is disabled with the reason, because the year is invented. On a phone the strip scrolls, starting at the latest week.
  • Punch card: circles are sized by area. In hours mode a recording is split across the hours it covers. The callout says busiest, and the row and column totals back it up. Its ring is ink, not orange. On a phone it turns sideways (hours down) and trims to the hours that have data.
  • Model usage: colour follows the engine or model between Totals and By month. Whisper is blue and Plaud ochre; qwen3.6 is blue, gemma ochre and claude-opus violet. claude-opus never gets private recordings (§7.4), so its count is lower.
  • Proposed --chart-1…3 for _brand.yml: light #236192 #B5832A #8A5BA8, dark #5A95CF #B98A30 #A07AC8. They pass the dataviz validator against the card colour in both modes: adjacent CVD ΔE at least 21, normal vision at least 22, and 3:1 or better. #6CA6D9 sits just outside the dark lightness band, so dark charts use #5A95CF.
  • Privacy: private recordings count in every total, but their titles never appear (the day details say "Private recording"). The private person shows as "1 private person".
  • The sample year is 312 records from seed 20261008, rolling 12 months ending today. It has Tuesday and Thursday lectures and Friday labs in term weeks, Wednesday office hours, a Monday sync, a Tuesday check-in, a fortnightly planning call, ad-hoc meetings, occasional weekend talks and private memos. Dec 21 – Jan 3 and Aug 3 – 23 are holidays with nothing recorded, which is what the empty-range state uses.
  • The real build uses shadcn charts (Recharts, with the brand tokens) for the bars: model usage, trends and speakers. The heatmap and punch card are plain SVG, as here. There is no chart library in this mockup.
  • The date control is a plain preset select here, because the picker has its own mockup. D focuses it. The route readout in the mockup bar shows the hash route that Back returns to.

Open questions for Dan

  • Heatmap steps. "0, under ½, 1, 2 and 4+" leaves ½–1 h with no step if read as five boxes. The mockup reads them as edges, which gives six boxes: 0, under ½, ½–1, 1–2, 2–4 and 4+. Is that right, or should two of them merge?
  • Count mode needs its own steps. The mockup uses 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5+ recordings.
  • Year stepper or date control? The stepper moves by calendar year and dims days outside the range, so "All time" (a rolling year) is split across 2025 and 2026. The alternative is a GitHub-style "past 12 months" page first.
  • Tag chips. The Library combines two tags with AND. These three chips barely overlap, so the mockup uses any-of. Should Insights keep AND?
  • Private people in Speakers. The spec says "who you meet most" both leaves out private-only people and masks people.private.yaml names, and by default those are the same people. The mockup shows them as one masked row. Should they be left out entirely?
  • Empty days in dark mode. --muted (#1C1A16) is darker than the card in dark mode, so empty days look like holes. The mockup uses --secondary (#2E2B26). Should it be a --heat-0 token?
  • Clock format. The callout uses 24-hour time ("Tue 14:00–16:00", as in the brief), but the spec's example is "Tuesdays 2–4 pm". Which should it be?
  • Week start. §12.6a says the date picker's week "starts on the same day as in Insights", but nothing sets that day. The mockup uses Monday. Should it be a config key?
