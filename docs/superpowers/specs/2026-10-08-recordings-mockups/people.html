Design notes

What the mockup shows

  • Tabs, per §12.6a: People · To link · Suggestions · Fix in Plaud · Plaud vs home. At build stage 3, Suggestions and Plaud vs home show as dashed "stage 6" placeholders, so you can see where they will go. The real app hides them until stage 6. Switch Build stage to 6 to review them working.
  • The badge on People counts what needs you: names to link, open Fix in Plaud items and, from stage 6, suggestions. It is blue, not orange, because orange already marks the active page (§12.6).
  • People:
    • Rows are sorted by when the person was last heard. Placeholders are collapsed under "Unnamed (3)".
    • Anyone in people.private.yaml shows a lock and "Private: local only" (§7.6). A group shows "Never fingerprinted" in place of the switch.
    • At stage 3, Recognise this voice only sets the rule. At stage 6, turning it off deletes their voice data, and the toast says so.
  • A person's page:
    • Aliases are editable. Adding "Sam" to Sam Lee links Plaud's "Sam" straight away, because an alias edit re-runs linking (§9.1.1).
    • Rename keeps the slug.
    • Merge with… lets you choose which name survives. Aliases are combined, and recognise stays on only if both people had it on (§7.6).
  • Forget is the app's one confirmation (§12.2): you type the person's name. The dialog lists the six scrub steps and what still holds the data, Plaud's cloud included (§7.6). It has no Undo, and it also clears the mockup's undo history.
  • To link:
    • Suggestions use Plaud's own voice key (embedding_key, §9.1.1). "Sam" points to Sam Lee because Plaud gave both the same key. "Alex K" and "alex kim" share a key, so they get "one person with…". "Dr. Lee" only shares a surname, so it gets no suggestion.
    • Selection works like Finder (§12.2): click, ⌘/Ctrl-click, Shift-click or the checkboxes. "One person from these 3" asks which name to show, offering a capitalised "Alex Kim" as well; the other names become aliases.
    • "Accept all suggested links" leaves out any row that needs your judgement (two voices, or no suggestion) and names what it left out.
    • "Alex" has two voices: the "Alex" in Office hours has Sam Lee's voice key. Split per recording saves that one recording as yours, with plaud_name_seen: "Alex", which is the example in §7.6. It also adds an item to Fix in Plaud.
  • Suggestions (stage 6):
    • Grouped by person, with their reference clip pinned at the top. Medium matches only, best first.
    • Space Y N S U work. Each decision plays the next clip.
    • Rows you have decided stay where they are, so the list never reshuffles. Courses (voice: off) and private recordings never appear here.
  • Fix in Plaud: each item shows Plaud's name against ours and the right name, with Copy. Open in Plaud ↗ and Done in Plaud move the item to "waiting for a re-sync". Re-sync now clears it.
  • Phone: the main nav becomes a bottom tab bar (§12), tables become stacked rows, dialogs become bottom sheets, and the key hints are hidden.

Spec gaps and questions for Dan

  • Fix in Plaud before stage 6. §7.6 fills it only from the yardstick's "Ours is right", which is stage 6, yet the tab is listed for stage 3. The mockup assumes that overriding a Plaud name yourself (a label, a span or a split, recorded in plaud_name_seen) adds an item from stage 3. Is that right? And is it still worth fixing Plaud once the subscription ends?
  • Case in linking. Aliases are unique ignoring case (§7.6), but nothing says whether matching Plaud names ignores case. If it does, "alex kim" never reaches To link once Alex Kim exists. Here it ignores case, and Alex Kim does not exist yet.
  • The evidence for two voices. "One Plaud name meaning two people" needs some evidence, and the spec doesn't say what. The mockup uses a differing embedding_key, which assumes Plaud's key is stable across recordings. Worth checking on real data.
  • Accept all and the random candidates. About 10% of Suggestions are random lower-scored candidates (§7.6, selection bias), so Accept all would accept them too. Sorting by score also puts them at the bottom, where they stand out. Proposal: interleave them unmarked, and leave them out of Accept all.
  • Three counters. Fix in Plaud is also in the Library's Review (N) (§12.1), and the orange approvals badge (§12.5) sits in the same top bar. Should there be one "needs you" number?
  • A private person in a non-private recording. Linking one there means moving them to people.yaml "after Dan confirms" (§7.6). That is a confirmation, which §12.2 otherwise rules out, and its design is still open. "Priya S." avoids the problem only because COURSE 101 has external_names: false.
  • Groups from courses. A group made from course recordings lands in people.private.yaml by default, so "Students" gets a lock. Is that intended for groups?
