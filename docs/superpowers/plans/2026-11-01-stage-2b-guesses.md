# Stage 2b: the build's guesses (for Dan to check while testing)

Stage 2b's Phases 2 and 3 were built straight through (Dan, 2026-11-01; Phase 1's Q&A §8), with the
spike sessions skipped. Where the spec left a Plaud detail to a spike, or left a call open, the
build took what `audio-router`'s `src/` and `tests/` show, or else the spec's own default, choosing
the reading that is most private and fails closed. Each such guess is a row below: what was
guessed, where it lives in the code, and how Dan would notice, while testing the app or on the
first real run, that it is wrong. A wrong guess is fixed where the row points; none of them can let
a recording in that should stay out.
