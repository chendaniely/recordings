# Opening of "We choose to go to the Moon" (Rice University, 1962)

## TL;DR
Kennedy begins his Rice University speech with formal greetings and a framing of the era:
knowledge is growing, and with it the sense of how much is still unknown.

## Key points
- Formal greetings to the university president, state and federal officials and guests.
- The era is cast as one of change and challenge, of hope and fear.
- Knowledge and ignorance are said to grow together, even as the number of scientists rises.
